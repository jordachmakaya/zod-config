# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project should be a practical, respectful place to collaborate. We expect
participants to:

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Keep discussions focused on the work.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Avoid harassment, insults, threats, and discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Harassment, intimidation, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Deliberately introducing malware, credential leaks, or unsafe code.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this code. Maintainers may also temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers through the
repository's available private reporting channels when available.
